## 🏛️ The 12 Constitutional Rules of PILL RED

### Rule 001 — Single-Source Sovereignty
`C:\PILL RED\` is the sole, authoritative home for the entire project. Everything we build, discover, test, reject, prove, falsify, modify, or decide belongs to `PILL RED`.

### Rule 002 — RED PILL DOCK
The native command and visual interface of PILL RED is officially named **RED PILL DOCK**. It is located at `C:\PILL RED\red_pill_dock\`.

### Rule 003 — No External Runtime Coupling
PILL RED cannot depend on another project's runtime, source tree, or execution configuration to operate. External code may be copied and rewritten inside `C:\PILL RED\`, but the PILL RED system stands completely alone.

### Rule 004 — Complete Provenance
Every substantive research or engineering event must have an unbroken, auditable record tracing:
$$\text{Event} \longrightarrow \text{Hypothesis} \longrightarrow \text{Experiment} \longrightarrow \text{Raw Input} \longrightarrow \text{Execution} \longrightarrow \text{Analysis} \longrightarrow \text{Discovery / Falsification} \longrightarrow \text{Telemetry}$$

### Rule 005 — Immutable Evidence
Raw experimental evidence is permanently preserved in machine-readable format (`evidence/BENCHMARK_RECORDS/`) rather than overwritten or discarded.

### Rule 006 — Bounded Claims
Empirical claims remain strictly scoped to their tested domain. Success on 16–48 round benchmarks does not imply infinite scaling or exponential search elimination.

### Rule 007 — Separation of Observation and Interpretation
Raw measurements (conflicts, decisions, timings), derived metrics (percentages), hypotheses, discoveries, and conclusions remain strictly distinguishable.

### Rule 008 — Falsification Is First-Class
Failed or invalid approaches (such as unitary rotations or synthetic 3D namespace embeddings) are preserved in `falsifications/` and `rejected/` as valuable discoveries about what does not work.

### Rule 009 — No Silent State
Important changes, mathematical formulations, and engineering states cannot exist solely in an AI session, terminal buffer, or developer memory. Everything is committed to the repository.

### Rule 010 — Dock Telemetry Must Have Provenance
Anything presented by `RED PILL DOCK` as scientific or engineering telemetry must identify its authoritative source record, test domain, and claim scope.

### Rule 011 — Master Status Is a Map, Not the Evidence
`PROJECT_MASTER_STATUS.md` points to authoritative evidence records; it does not replace or overwrite them.

### Rule 012 — Reproducibility
Every claimed result must have enough code, configuration, parameter manifests, and random seed data inside `C:\PILL RED\` to allow independent auditing and exact reproduction.

### Rule 013 — Pre-Experimental Theoretical Literature Reconciliation & Route Classification
No new computational experiment may be designated as a $P = NP$ investigation until its underlying mathematical mechanism has first been reconciled against the relevant established literature and classified as either known (Category A), an engineering implementation (Category B), provably bounded/blocked by a formal complexity barrier (Category C), or genuinely unexplored (Category D). No experimental implementation may proceed toward $P = NP$ claims unless a Category D theoretical opening is formally established.
