### 🌐 Territory 1: Discrete Topology & Configuration Spaces

| Analytical Dimension | Rigorous Assessment |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. What is Actually Proven** | • Computing Betti numbers $\beta_k(K)$ or Euler characteristic $\chi(K)$ on generic finite simplicial complexes is $\#\mathbf{P}$-complete (*Babson, Bjorner, Linial 1999*).<br>• Finding optimal discrete Morse gradient vector fields on general simplicial complexes is $\mathbf{NP}$-hard via reduction to collapsibility (*Lewiner et al. 2003*), though polynomial on restricted sub-classes (e.g. 2-manifolds). |
| **2. What is Merely Suspected** | • *Suspected:* Any topological encoding of CNF formulas must suffer from the $\#\mathbf{P}$-hardness of cell counting or the $\mathbf{NP}$-hardness of Morse optimization.<br>• *Reality:* It is unproven whether specialized non-generic complexes derived from CNF structure possess alternate polynomial topological invariants. |
| **3. Theorem Required to Close Route** | A formal proof that *for every polynomial-time computable topological functor mapping CNF formulas to cell complexes, computing any topological invariant that separates SAT from UNSAT is $\mathbf{NP}$-hard.* (Currently unproven). |
| **4. What Constitutes a D1–D6 Opening** | A topological complex $K(\mathcal{F})$ constructible in $O(\text{poly}(n))$ time whose 1-homology or discrete Morse boundary operator over a finite field computes global satisfiability without full cell enumeration. |
| **Current Classification** | **OPEN TERRITORY — KNOWN OBSTACLES ON INVESTIGATED MECHANISMS; NO CATEGORY-D CANDIDATE IDENTIFIED.** |

### ⚡ Territory 2: Non-Equilibrium Continuous Dynamical Systems

| Analytical Dimension | Rigorous Assessment |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. What is Actually Proven** | • The Blum–Shub–Smale (BSS 1989) model establishes computational complexity over the real numbers $\mathbb{R}$ and proves that $\mathbf{NP}_{\mathbb{R}}$-complete problems exist.<br>• Non-linear dynamical systems and continuous ODEs can exhibit undecidable reachability and chaotic sensitivity (*Moore 1990, Branicky 1995, Bournez et al. 2007*). |
| **2. What is Merely Suspected** | • *Suspected:* Every non-linear ODE embedding SAT requires exponentially fine precision ($\epsilon = 2^{-\Omega(n)}$) or exponential integration time ($T = 2^{\Omega(n)}$) due to saddle traps.<br>• *Reality:* This precision/time lower bound has not been established as a universal theorem for all continuous dynamical systems. |
| **3. Theorem Required to Close Route** | A formal theorem establishing that *for any polynomial-degree continuous ODE system $\dot{\mathbf{x}} = \mathbf{F}(\mathbf{x})$ whose attractors decide SAT, either the basin convergence time is $2^{\Omega(n)}$ or numerical integration requires $2^{\Omega(n)}$ bits of precision.* (Currently unproven). |
| **4. What Constitutes a D1–D6 Opening** | A continuous non-linear ODE flow $\dot{\mathbf{x}} = \mathbf{F}(\mathbf{x})$ constructible in $O(\text{poly}(n))$ time whose Lyapunov spectrum or global energy attractor separates SAT from UNSAT in $O(\text{poly}(n))$ integration time with $O(\text{poly}(n))$ bits of numerical precision. |
| **Current Classification** | **OPEN TERRITORY — NO CATEGORY-D CANDIDATE IDENTIFIED.** |

### 🧩 Territory 3: Cross-Framework Heterogeneous Couplings

| Analytical Dimension | Rigorous Assessment |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. What is Actually Proven** | • Specific unsatisfiable formula families (such as Tseitin formulas on expanders and random 3-CNF at the phase transition) admit exponential Resolution lower bounds (*Urquhart 1987, Chvátal-Szemerédi 1988*).<br>• Ordinary linear $\mathbb{F}_2$ elimination does not directly decide general nonlinear polynomial constraints without higher-degree monomial representations.<br>• Level-$k$ SoS requires $k = \Omega(n)$ rounds on specific hard families (*Schoenebeck 2008*); $k$-WL is blind to satisfiability on specific CFI formula families for any fixed tuple dimension $k < \Omega(n)$ (*Grohe et al. 2024*). |
| **2. What is Merely Suspected** | • *Suspected:* Coupling two limited frameworks $A + B$ must be constrained by the union of their individual lower bounds.<br>• *Reality:* In computational complexity, hybrid systems can produce capabilities that neither subsystem possesses independently (e.g. interactive proofs $\text{IP} = \text{PSPACE}$, or SAT+CAS solving open mathematical conjectures). |
| **3. Theorem Required to Close Route** | A formal structural composition theorem proving that *for a specific hybrid coupling $(A \otimes B)$, the lower-bound certificates of framework $A$ and framework $B$ compose to enforce exponential proof complexity on the combined system.* (Currently unproven in generality). |
| **4. What Constitutes a D1–D6 Opening** | A hybrid coupling mechanism (e.g. algebraic elimination feeding global topological constraints) with a formal bridge theorem demonstrating why the coupled state space does not explode and how the joint operator escapes the individual lower bounds of both subsystems. |
