## 3. Core Scientific Discoveries

### 1. 🔬 Mathematical & Empirical Falsification of Unitary Degeneracy Lifting (Rep C)
- For any Hermitian Laplacian $\mathbf{L}$, the unitary operator generated by $\mathbf{L}$ is $U(\theta) = e^{i\theta \mathbf{L}}$.
- Because $U(\theta)$ is an analytic function of $\mathbf{L}$, it strictly commutes with $\mathbf{L}$:
  $$\mathbf{L}' = U(\theta) \mathbf{L} U(\theta)^\dagger = \mathbf{L} U(\theta) U(\theta)^\dagger = \mathbf{L}$$
- Empirical measurement on the 16-round SHA-256 Laplacian verified:
  - Unitarity Residual: $\|U^\dagger U - I\|_F = 2.49 \times 10^{-14}$
  - Commutativity Residual: $\|U \mathbf{L} U^\dagger - \mathbf{L}\|_F = 1.40 \times 10^{-13}$

- **Definitive Conclusion:** Unitary conjugation generated by $\mathbf{L}$ cannot alter the spectrum, cannot lift eigenspace degeneracy, and cannot create new structural information.
