## 2. Mathematical Proof of Falsification
Let $\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{B}^T \mathbf{B} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ be a symmetric, positive semi-definite Graph Laplacian with spectral decomposition $\mathbf{L} = V \Lambda V^T$.
The unitary operator generated by $\mathbf{L}$ is defined by the matrix exponential:
$$U(\theta) = e^{i\theta \mathbf{L}} = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{(i\theta)^k}{k!} \mathbf{L}^k = V e^{i\theta \Lambda} V^\dagger$$
Conjugating $\mathbf{L}$ by $U(\theta)$ yields:
$$\mathbf{L}' = U(\theta) \mathbf{L} U(\theta)^\dagger = \left(V e^{i\theta \Lambda} V^\dagger\right) \left(V \Lambda V^\dagger\right) \left(V e^{-i\theta \Lambda} V^\dagger\right) = V \left(e^{i\theta \Lambda} \Lambda e^{-i\theta \Lambda}\right) V^\dagger = V \Lambda V^\dagger = \mathbf{L}$$
Because diagonal matrices commute, $e^{i\theta \Lambda} \Lambda e^{-i\theta \Lambda} \equiv \Lambda$. Therefore:
$$[U(\theta), \mathbf{L}] = 0 \implies \mathbf{L}' \equiv \mathbf{L}$$
Unitary conjugation generated by $\mathbf{L}$ strictly commutes with $\mathbf{L}$, leaving its eigenvalues, eigenspaces, and coordinate projections completely unchanged.
